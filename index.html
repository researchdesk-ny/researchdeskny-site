ResearchDeskNY

An idea incubator, based in New York.

ResearchDeskNY is the idea incubator of Adrian Szczechura, an independent builder in New York City — the place his projects live while they grow, from first sketch to working thing. Every project below started with something ordinary: packages with nowhere safe to land, books headed for the trash, care that insurance refused to pay for.

One incubator, many ideas. Each project has its own page.

Buildings rent their amenity access to non-residents — starting with safe package receiving for neighbors whose buildings have none. Luxury market, Upper West Side pilot.
