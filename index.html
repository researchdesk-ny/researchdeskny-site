ResearchDeskNY

An idea incubator, based in New York.

ResearchDeskNY is the idea incubator of Adrian Szczechura, an independent builder in New York City — the place his projects live while they grow, from first sketch to working thing. Every project here started with something ordinary: a gap nobody was filling, a refusal that made no sense, a price that didn't add up.
