ResearchDeskNY

Ideas from a doorman in New York.

This is the project desk of Adrian Szczechura — a working doorman on Manhattan’s Upper West Side who builds things between shifts. Every project below started as something he saw at work: packages with nowhere safe to land, books headed for the trash, care that insurance refused to pay for.

One desk, many ideas. Each project has its own page.

Porter

Buildings rent their amenity access to non-residents — starting with safe package receiving for neighbors who have no doorman of their own. Luxury market, Upper West Side pilot.
