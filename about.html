About Adrian Szczechura

Adrian Szczechura is an independent builder in New York City — and the person behind everything on this site.

The ideas here come from ordinary New York life: packages that pile up in buildings with nowhere safe to put them, books — boxes of them — thrown away when an apartment turns over, care that insurance refused to cover. Porter, Book Rescue, and the Health Legacy Coin each started as one of those observations.

The projects

  • Porter — buildings rent amenity access to non-residents; packages first, Upper West Side pilot.
  • Merchant Wars — a legal-goods trading game across seven ports; a playable demo exists, and a grant application is in with the Awesome Foundation.
  • Health Legacy Coin — a published concept paper for a nonprofit health savings coin, written under his name.
  • Book Rescue — Second Spine — paying building staff to save thrown-away books instead of landfilling them.

ResearchDeskNY is his idea incubator: one address for all of it, from the first sketch onward.
