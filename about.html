About Adrian Szczechura

Adrian Szczechura is a doorman in New York City — and the builder of everything on this site.

Working the door of a Manhattan residential building turns out to be a front-row seat for ideas. You see the packages that pile up for neighbors who have no doorman of their own. You see the books — boxes of them — carried out to the trash when an apartment turns over. You hear, in the lobby, what insurance refused to cover this month. Porter, Book Rescue, and the Health Legacy Coin each started as one of those moments.

Adrian also sells on eBay as basedinnyc — largely remarkable objects rescued from the same stream of things New Yorkers leave behind, with honest condition notes and a 100% positive feedback record he intends to keep.

The projects

  • Porter — buildings rent amenity access to non-residents; packages first, Upper West Side pilot.
  • Merchant Wars — a legal-goods trading game across seven ports; a playable demo exists, and a grant application is in with the Awesome Foundation.
  • Health Legacy Coin — a published concept paper for a nonprofit health savings coin, written under his name.
  • Book Rescue — Second Spine — paying building staff to save thrown-away books instead of landfilling them.

ResearchDeskNY is his desk: one address for all of it.
