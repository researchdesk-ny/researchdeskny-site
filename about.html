About Adrian Szczechura

Adrian Szczechura is an independent builder in New York City — and the person behind everything on this site.

The ideas here come from ordinary New York life — most sharply, care that insurance refused to cover. The Health Legacy Coin started as one of those observations. The rest of the sketchbook stays closed until each idea is ready to be shown.

The projects

  • Health Legacy Coin — a published concept paper for a nonprofit health savings coin, written under his name.
  • Merchant Wars — a legal-goods trading game across seven ports — a nostalgia play on the classic Dope Wars, rebuilt drug-free. A playable demo runs on this site.

ResearchDeskNY is his idea incubator: one address for all of it, from the first sketch onward.
